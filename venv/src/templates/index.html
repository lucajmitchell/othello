Othello

The aim of the game is to have the mojority of the discs showing your own colour when the game ends. A move consists of "outflanking" your opponent's disc(s), then flipping the outflanked disc(s) to your colour. To outflank means to place a disc on the board so that your opponent's sequence of disc(s) is bordered at each end by a disc of your colour. (A "sequence" may be made up of one or more discs).
